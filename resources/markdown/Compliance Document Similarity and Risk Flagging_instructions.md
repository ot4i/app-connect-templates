## Using the template

1. Click **Use this template** to start using the template.
1. Connect to your [CMIS account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-cmis).

    **Note:** The flow polls for newly created **Document** objects in the configured CMIS repository folder. It triggers automatically whenever a new document is uploaded.
1. On the CMIS "New document" event node, select the **Repository** and the **Folder** to monitor for new document uploads.
1. Click on the **Google Gemini** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Google Gemini account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-google-gemini).

    - On the Google Gemini "Analyze document content" node, confirm the **Model ID** (for example, `models/gemini-2.5-pro`).
    - The node receives the binary document content downloaded from CMIS and extracts its text.
1. Click on the **IBM watsonx.ai** node (Generate embeddings), and if you're not already connected, connect to your [IBM watsonx.ai account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-watsonxai).

    - Select or confirm the **Model ID** for embedding generation (for example, `ibm/granite-embedding-278m-multilingual`).
    - Enter your watsonx.ai **Project ID**.
    - The node uses the text extracted by Google Gemini as its input.
1. Click on the **Qdrant** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Qdrant account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-qdrant).

    - On the Qdrant "Query points in batch" node, select or confirm the **Collection** to search against — this collection holds your compliance knowledge base (for example, `compliance_docs_watsonx`).
    - The search applies a minimum similarity score threshold of **0.70** and filters for documents with a `risk_score` of **7 or above** in your collection.
    - The embeddings generated by watsonx.ai are passed as the query vector.
1. The flow uses an **If** condition to check whether any matching points were returned by Qdrant.

    - **If matches are found**, a weighted risk score is calculated from the similarity scores and stored risk scores of the matched documents. The flow then branches further based on this score:

        - **Weighted risk score ≥ 7 (High risk):** A Salesforce case is created with **Status: Escalated** and **Priority: Low**, and a high-risk compliance alert email is sent via Gmail.
        - **Weighted risk score ≥ 5 (Medium risk):** A Salesforce case is created with **Status: Escalated** and **Priority: Medium**, and a medium-risk compliance alert email is sent via Gmail.
        - **Weighted risk score < 5 (Low risk):** A Salesforce case is created with **Status: Escalated** and **Priority: High**, and a low-risk compliance alert email is sent via Gmail.

    - **If no matches are found**, the flow ends without creating a case.
1. On each **Salesforce Create case** node, confirm the connection to your [Salesforce account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-salesforce). The case subject is set to `COMPLIANCE RISK` and the supplied name includes the document filename and its computed risk score.
1. On each **Gmail Send email** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Gmail account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-gmail).

    - Update the **To** field on each Gmail node with the email address that should receive compliance alerts.
1. Click **Done**.
1. To start the flow, in the banner click **Start flow**.

    When the flow is running and a new document is uploaded to the monitored CMIS folder, the flow downloads the document, extracts its text using Google Gemini, generates embeddings with IBM watsonx.ai, and queries Qdrant for semantically similar documents with high risk scores. A weighted risk score is computed and the document is classified as high, medium, or low risk. A Salesforce case is created and a compliance alert email is sent via Gmail according to the risk level.


## Test your flow

1. Upload a compliance-related document (for example, a PDF or Word file) to the CMIS folder you configured as the trigger source.
1. Wait a few moments for the flow to poll and complete.
1. In Salesforce, verify that a new **Case** has been created with the subject **COMPLIANCE RISK**, containing the document filename and its computed risk score in the case description and supplied name.
1. Check your inbox for a compliance alert email with a subject in the format **[COMPLIANCE ALERT] \<filename\> - Risk Score \<score\>/10**, indicating the detected risk level (High, Medium, or Low).
1. Adjust the risk thresholds or the Qdrant collection as needed to tune sensitivity for your compliance knowledge base.
