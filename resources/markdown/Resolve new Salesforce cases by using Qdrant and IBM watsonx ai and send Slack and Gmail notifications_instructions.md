## Using the template

1. Click **Use this template** to start using the template.
1. Connect to your [Salesforce account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-salesforce).

    **Note:** The flow is triggered automatically whenever a new **Case** is created in Salesforce.
1. On the Salesforce "New case" event node, confirm the trigger is set to listen for the **Case** object being created.
1. On the Salesforce "Retrieve cases" node, confirm the filter uses the case **Id** from the trigger to retrieve the full case record.
1. Click on the first **IBM watsonx.ai** node (Generate embeddings), and if you're not already connected, connect to your [IBM watsonx.ai account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-watsonxai).

    - Select or confirm the **Model ID** to use for embedding generation (for example, `ibm/granite-embedding-278m-multilingual`).
    - Enter your watsonx.ai **Project ID**.
    - The node uses the case **Description** retrieved in the previous step as input.
1. Click on the **Qdrant** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Qdrant account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-qdrant).

    - On the Qdrant "Query points in batch" node, select the **Collection** to search against — this is the collection that holds your support knowledge base.
    - The embeddings generated by watsonx.ai are passed as the query vector for the semantic search.
1. Click on the second **IBM watsonx.ai** node (Generate text), and confirm the connection.

    - The node constructs a prompt combining the case **Title** and **Description** as the question, along with the context retrieved from Qdrant.
    - Select or confirm the **Model ID** for text generation (for example, `meta-llama/llama-3-3-70b-instruct`).
    - Enter your watsonx.ai **Project ID**.
1. On the Salesforce "Update case" node, confirm the AI-generated answer is mapped to the **Comments** field of the case identified by the trigger **Id**.
1. Click on the **Slack** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Slack account](https://ibm.biz/aasslack).

    - On the Slack "Send message" node, select the **Destination type** (for example, `channel`) and the **Send to** value (the channel where case analysis notifications should be posted).
1. Click on the **Gmail** node, and if you're not already connected, connect to your [Gmail account](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/app-connect/saas?topic=apps-gmail).

    - On the Gmail "Send email" node, confirm the **To**, **Subject**, and **Body** fields. The body includes the case ID and the AI-generated analysis.
1. Click **Done**.
1. To start the flow, in the banner click **Start flow**.

    When the flow is running and a new case is created in Salesforce, the flow retrieves the case description, generates embeddings using IBM watsonx.ai, and queries Qdrant for relevant context from the knowledge base. That context is used by IBM watsonx.ai to generate a resolution comment, which is posted back to the Salesforce case. A notification is then sent to the configured Slack channel and an email summary is sent via Gmail.


## Test your flow

1. In Salesforce, create a new **Case** with a descriptive **Subject** and **Description**.
1. Wait a few moments for the flow to trigger and complete.
1. Open the case in Salesforce and verify that an AI-generated comment has been added to the **Comments** field.
1. Check the configured Slack channel for a notification message containing the case ID and the AI analysis.
1. Check your inbox for an email with the subject **New Case Created – AI Analysis Completed**, containing the case ID and the AI-generated analysis.
